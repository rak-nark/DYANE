# What is Dynatrace

 Latest Dynatrace 

 Explanation 

 5-min read 
- Updated on Jun 03, 2026 

Dynatrace® is the agentic AI platform for unified observability, security, and automation. The platform continuously senses system behavior, identifies what matters, and autonomously acts across applications, infrastructure, cloud platforms, and AI workloads.

Powered by predictive, causal, and generative AI through Dynatrace Intelligence, the Dynatrace platform turns high-volume telemetry into precise insights and coordinated actions, reducing operational risk, improving reliability, and connecting technical decisions to business impact. 

 What is Dynatrace in 15 minutes 

## What can you do with Dynatrace? 

With Dynatrace, you can: 
- **Understand complex systems**: Automatically discover and map applications, infrastructure, cloud services, dependencies, and user journeys across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.
- **Explain cause and impact**: Use Dynatrace Intelligence to identify root cause, predict issues, and understand how technical events affect users and business outcomes.
- **Automate operations**: Turn observability and security insights into automated workflows across DevOps, site reliability engineering (SRE), platform engineering, and security teams.
- **Operate AI systems with confidence**: Monitor, optimize, and govern LLMs, GenAI workloads, and agentic workflows with full visibility, performance insights, and risk controls through AI observability.
 
## Platform capabilities

Dynatrace addresses a comprehensive range of use cases across observability, security, analytics, and automation, making it suitable for organizations of all sizes across various industries. 

 Dynatrace Intelligence platform architecture, including data collection, analytics, and automation layers across observability, security, and business domains 

### Application Observability

*Monitor, analyze, and optimize application performance across distributed and cloud-native environments.*

The Dynatrace platform provides deep visibility into application behavior using PurePath® distributed tracing, code-level diagnostics, service dependency mapping, and AI-driven performance insights. With native OpenTelemetry support, teams can instrument once and immediately enrich that data with the platform's full AI and automation capabilities. The result is faster identification of latency, errors, and bottlenecks across microservices and APIs, continuously improving reliability and user experience. For details, see Application Observability .
### Log Analytics

*Analyze massive volumes of log data with context and speed.*

Powered by Grail™, the Dynatrace platform correlates logs with metrics, traces, and events to provide instant answers and remove the overhead of traditional log management. Teams can troubleshoot faster, reduce costs, and gain deeper insights into system behavior. For details, see Log Analytics .
### AI/LLM Observability

*Operate AI systems with performance, transparency, and control.*

The Dynatrace platform delivers end-to-end visibility into large language model (LLM) pipelines, inference services, AI orchestration layers, and multi-agent systems. Tracked metrics include latency, cost, quality signals, and runtime dependencies. Teams can optimize model efficiency, reduce operational risk, and ensure AI workloads meet reliability and compliance standards at scale. For details, see AI Observability for generative AI and LLM models with Dynatrace .
### Application Security

*Identify and mitigate risks across applications and runtime environments.*

The Dynatrace platform provides continuous application security insights, including runtime vulnerability detection, code-level risk analysis, and behavioral threat detection. Teams can prioritize vulnerabilities based on real-world impact and integrate security directly into DevOps workflows. For details, see Application Security .
### Digital Experience

*Deliver flawless digital experiences across web, mobile, and connected channels.*

The Dynatrace platform combines real user monitoring (RUM), synthetic monitoring, Session Replay, and user journey analytics to connect technical performance with customer experience and business outcomes. For details, see Digital Experience .
### Business Observability

*Connect technical performance to business outcomes.*

The Dynatrace platform links telemetry data to business KPIs such as revenue, conversion, and customer satisfaction. Teams can make real-time decisions and prioritize based on business impact. For details, see Business Observability .
### Developer Experience

*Accelerate software delivery with observability-driven automation.*

The Dynatrace platform integrates with CI/CD pipelines to validate deployments, detect regressions, and enforce quality gates based on performance and security metrics. Teams can release faster while reducing risk. For details, see Software Delivery and Live Debugger .
### Infrastructure Observability

*Understand the health, performance, and capacity of infrastructure across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.*

The Dynatrace platform automatically discovers infrastructure components, maps real-time topology, and applies predictive analytics to identify potential issues before they impact services. Teams can plan capacity proactively, resolve incidents faster, and optimize cloud resource utilization. For details, see Infrastructure Observability .

## How Dynatrace works 

Powerful core technologies combine, allowing the Dynatrace platform to deliver analytics and automation for unified observability and security. Dynatrace Intelligence connects these capabilities, providing the predictive, causal, and generative AI that powers agentic operations, allowing teams to move from insight to action automatically, at scale. 

### Dynatrace Intelligence

Dynatrace Intelligence uniquely combines predictive AI, causal AI, and generative AI for observability, security, and business use cases. 

### AppEngine

Create and share custom apps that leverage insights from all your observability, security, and business data. 

### AutomationEngine

Accelerate digital transformation with simple yet powerful automations driven by observability and security insights. 

### Grail

Grail™ is a causational data lakehouse with a massively parallel processing (MPP) analytics engine that leverages Dynatrace Query Language (DQL) for instant, AI-powered, context-rich analytics. 

### Smartscape

Smartscape® dynamic environment-topology mapping visualizes the dynamic relationships among all application components across every tier. 
### OpenPipeline

Dynatrace OpenPipeline manages all aspects of Dynatrace's unified ingestion with powerful, scalable, and flexible stream processing and data extraction options. 

### OneAgent

OneAgent® services automatically discover, activate, and instrument applications, microservices, infrastructure, and any dependencies in modern cloud environments. 
## FAQ 
 What are the key features of Dynatrace? 

The Dynatrace platform offers comprehensive observability and automation features, including: 
- Agentic AI platform for observability across applications, infrastructure, logs, and user experience 
- Unified data lakehouse (Grail) and query language (DQL) 
- Automatic discovery and instrumentation via OneAgent® 
- Causal, predictive, and generative AI through Dynatrace Intelligence 
- Integrated application security and threat detection 
- Automation workflows and custom applications 
- Business and digital experience analytics 
 How does Dynatrace work? 

The Dynatrace platform operates through three core layers: 
- **Data collection:** OneAgent, OpenPipeline, and OpenTelemetry capture telemetry across applications, infrastructure, cloud services, and third-party tools.
- **Data intelligence:** Grail unifies and analyzes data in a causal data lakehouse.
- **AI and automation:** Dynatrace Intelligence identifies patterns, root causes, and risks, while AutomationEngine executes workflows and responses.

Smartscape® topology mapping provides context about relationships between components, allowing precise impact analysis and root cause identification. What types of industries use Dynatrace?
